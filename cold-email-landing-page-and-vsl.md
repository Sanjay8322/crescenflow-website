# Landing Page + VSL: DFY Cold Email, Pay Per Showed Call

Offer: `cold-email-lead-gen`. Built on Daniel Fazio's landing page framework: the headline is the offer, the VSL's first line repeats the headline, then "how it works," then the CTA. The page has no extras.

Facts used (from `.claude/offers/cold-email-lead-gen.md`): showed calls (never "booked" or "qualified"), never "guaranteed", no invented case studies. Items in `[brackets]` are for you to fill in or decide.

---

## Landing page

**Headline (H1)**
> Performance-Based Cold Email: Pay Only For Calls That Actually Show Up

**Sub-line (optional, one sentence)**
> We build the list, set up the sending, run the campaign and handle the replies. You only pay per showed call.

**Video**: the VSL below (autoplay off, captions on, 3 minutes or less)

**Button (below the video)**
> Book a Call With Me

Link to your calendar. Later, once you have case studies, swap this for an application form (revenue, sales team size, who you sell to).

**Footer, one line, small**
> Built for B2B companies selling to decision-makers [with at least 5,000 reachable contacts].

That's the whole page. If you add anything, add proof below the button, not above the video.

## VSL script (about 3 minutes, around 430 words)

**[0:00 Intro. Same as the headline.]**

Hey, if you're a business owner and you want performance-based cold email, where you only pay for calls that actually show up, listen up. This video explains exactly how it works and how to get started.

I'm [Name], and I'll keep this simple. I'm going to show you what we do, what it costs, and what you pay for.

**[0:25 How it works. Show a doc or screen share, one visual per step.]**

First, we don't send from your main domain, because that would risk landing your regular email in spam. So we buy separate domains for sending, and set up two inboxes on each one. We warm those inboxes up before the first email goes out.

Second, we build your list. Say you sell to [example buyer type, e.g. commercial roofing contractors]. We build a list of every decision-maker we can reach in that market, and we verify every email address, so we aren't sending to dead inboxes.

Third, we write the email sequence and load the list in. Three short emails, written for the people you want to reach. The wording rotates automatically so the campaign doesn't look identical in every inbox.

Fourth, we run it. We send in stages, watch deliverability, and adjust as we go.

Fifth, we handle every reply. When someone's interested, we book them straight onto your calendar.

That's it. List, sending, sequence, replies, calls on your calendar.

**[1:45 The offer, plainly.]**

Here's the offer. You don't pay us a retainer. You pay per showed call, [$200–300] each, only when a contact who fits the targeting we agreed on actually joins the meeting. No show, no charge.

**[2:10 Who it's for. This filters.]**

This works best when you sell to businesses, your buyers are reachable by work email, and there's a big enough market to send to. We look for at least five thousand decision-makers we can email. If your market is smaller than that, I'll tell you honestly on the call, because it won't work well for either of us.

**[2:35 Proof. Skip until you have it. Then use this slot.]**

`[Insert 1–2 real results with the client's name and their screenshot or login, once you have them.]`

**[2:40 CTA.]**

If you want me to do this for your business, completely on a performance basis where you pay per showed call, click the button below and book a call with me. On the call I'll look at who you sell to, check whether the market is big enough, and tell you straight whether this is a fit.

## Notes

- **Repetition is deliberate.** "Pay per showed call" appears in the headline, the first line, the offer section and the CTA.
- **The "who it's for" section is a soft qualifier.** It stands in for the application form until you have the proof to use one.
- **No setup fee.** Unlike Fazio's tech fee, this offer has none. If you add one later, itemize the costs: domains, inboxes, list, sequencer, and the total.
- **No case studies yet?** Fazio says to be honest. If it applies, add one sentence after the intro: "I don't have a long list of case studies yet, which is why the offer is built so you carry as little risk as possible."
- **Match your outreach to the page.** The DM or email should say the same thing as the headline, so prospects who Google your sending domain land on a page that repeats it.
